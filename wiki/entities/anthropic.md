# Anthropic

AI safety company. Founded 2021 by former OpenAI researchers (Dario Amodei, Daniela Amodei, others). Creator of the Claude model family (including Claude Sonnet 4.6, Claude Opus 4.6, Claude Haiku 4.5).

## Relevance to this wiki

- **[[sources/ai-2027]]**: Dario Amodei (CEO) quoted predicting AI could surpass human intelligence by 2027 (January 2025).
- **[[sources/garrytan-thin-harness-fat-skills]]**: On March 31, 2026, Anthropic accidentally published the full Claude Code source (~512,000 lines) to the npm registry. [[entities/garry-tan|Garry Tan]] reviewed it and reports it confirms the [[concepts/thin-harness-fat-skills|thin harness, fat skills]] architecture: live repo context, prompt caching, purpose-built tools, context bloat minimization, structured session memory, parallel sub-agents.
- **This vault itself** runs on Claude Code (Claude Sonnet 4.6) — Anthropic's model is the LLM maintaining this wiki.

## AGI timeline stance

Dario Amodei (Jan 2025): "AI could surpass human intelligence by 2027." Consistent with Anthropic's long-standing belief that transformative AI is coming soon — central to their safety-first positioning (preparing for near-term powerful AI rather than treating it as decades away).

## Safety positioning

Anthropic explicitly frames itself as a safety-focused lab building powerful AI because "if powerful AI is coming regardless, it's better to have safety-focused labs at the frontier." This creates an inherent tension: accelerating development while claiming safety focus.
