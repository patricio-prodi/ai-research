# Google

Multinational technology company. In the AI research wiki, Google is relevant as the originator of [[entities/a2ui|A2UI]], the A2A Protocol (donated to the Linux Foundation), and Gemini models/Enterprise. Key internal teams contributing to agentic UI include the A2UI Team, Opal, Gemini Enterprise, Flutter, and AI Powered Google.

## AI / agentic products referenced in this wiki

- **[[entities/a2ui|A2UI]]** — open-source format for agent-driven generative UI (Dec 2025)
- **A2A Protocol** — inter-agent communication protocol, donated to Linux Foundation; see [[concepts/a2a-protocol]]
- **Opal** — AI mini-app builder; hundreds of thousands of users; early adopter of A2UI
- **Gemini Enterprise** — enterprise AI platform integrating A2UI for dynamic workflow UIs
- **Flutter / GenUI SDK** — multi-platform UI framework; uses A2UI as the server-agent → client UI declaration format
- **Gemini** — LLM family; used in A2UI demos (restaurant finder, landscaping form generation)
