# LangChain

A prominent Python [[agent-framework]] boasting the highest download volume and an ecosystem of over 1,000 integrations. 

## Characteristics
- Uses a ReAct-style tool-calling loop.
- Best suited for single-agent, linear tool-calling workflows and rapid prototyping.
- While its breadth is unmatched, it has faced criticism for poor debuggability due to high-level abstractions. 
- The underlying engine for more complex stateful workflows is its sister project, [[LangGraph]].
