# CrewAI

A Python-based [[agent-framework]] designed for building role-based multi-agent systems.

## Characteristics
- Models systems similarly to human teams, with agents assigned specific roles, goals, and backstories.
- Allows for extremely rapid prototyping of multi-agent delegation.
- Known limitations include difficult debugging (black-box abstractions) and potential token cost blowouts due to a lack of built-in token budget limits for autonomous delegation loops.
