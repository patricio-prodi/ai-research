# n8n

A visual workflow automation platform that incorporates AI agent capabilities.

## Characteristics
- Considered a "hybrid framework", offering a drag-and-drop editor connecting over 400 prebuilt nodes.
- Excellent for non-technical teams building business automations.
- May become unwieldy for highly complex, dynamic routing.
