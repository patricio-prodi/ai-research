# OpenAI

AI research lab and company. Founded 2015. Largest AI company by public visibility; creator of GPT series, ChatGPT, DALL-E, Codex.

## Relevance to this wiki

Appears across multiple sources:

- **[[llm-knowledge-bases-explained]]**: [[entities/andrej-karpathy|Karpathy]] is a founding researcher at OpenAI. GPT-4 and Claude cited as the capable LLMs that enable knowledge base workflows.
- **[[sources/ai-2027]]**: Written by ex-OpenAI researchers. Sam Altman (CEO) quoted predicting superintelligence "in the true sense of the word" soon.
- **[[sources/agentic-supply-chain-deloitte]]**: OpenAI's GPT models implicitly underlie many of the agentic AI deployments described.

## Key people (mentioned in sources)

- **Sam Altman** — CEO. Predicted AGI/superintelligence in near term (early 2025 blog post).
- **Andrej Karpathy** — Founding researcher (departed to work independently).

## AGI timeline stance

Sam Altman (2025): OpenAI is "setting its sights on superintelligence in the true sense of the word." In the same period, stated AGI could arrive within years.
