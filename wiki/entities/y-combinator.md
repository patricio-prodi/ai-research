# Y Combinator

Startup accelerator based in San Francisco. One of the most influential early-stage investors; alumni include Airbnb, Stripe, Dropbox, OpenAI. Current president: [[entities/garry-tan|Garry Tan]].

## Relevance to this wiki

- **[[sources/garrytan-thin-harness-fat-skills]]**: Tan describes teaching the [[concepts/thin-harness-fat-skills|thin harness, fat skills]] architecture at YC. He uses a projected 6,000-founder Startup School event (July 2026) as the primary case study for [[concepts/diarization|diarization]], [[concepts/skill-files|skill files]], and agent-driven matching at scale.

## Key context

- **Startup School**: Annual event for founders; the July 2026 case study describes scaling founder matching from 200 (human-manageable) to 6,000 (requires agentic systems) using the thin harness architecture.
- YC's experience with agentic AI tooling is framed as a reproducible pattern applicable to "every discipline and walk of life of knowledge work."

## YC application (from Hendrick / Conveo)

- **[[sources/hendrick-yc-application-tips]]**: Practitioner advice on the YC application process from Hendrick at Conveo. Key themes: invest in teams not ideas, reward velocity over planning, penalize jargon and evasion. See [[concepts/yc-application]] for the full playbook.
