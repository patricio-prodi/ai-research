# AGI Timelines

Forecasts and evidence about when Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) or superhuman AI will arrive, and what "arrival" means.

## Current best estimates (as of 2026-04)

| Source | Estimate | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| AI 2027 project | Modal: 2027; median: somewhat later | Ex-[[entities/openai\|OpenAI]] researchers; right-skewed distribution |
| Sam Altman (OpenAI CEO) | "Superintelligence in the true sense" soon | Blog post, early 2025 |
| Demis Hassabis (Google DeepMind CEO) | Within 5 years | 2025 interview |
| Dario Amodei ([[entities/anthropic\|Anthropic]] CEO) | Could surpass human intelligence by 2027 | Jan 2025 |

Industry CEO consensus as of early 2025: AGI within ~5 years (i.e., by 2030 at the latest).

## Defining AGI

The term is contested. [[sources/ai-2027]] uses "superhuman AI" — AI that exceeds human performance across most cognitive tasks. Sam Altman uses "superintelligence in the true sense of the word." The [[entities/anthropic|Anthropic]] framing tends toward "AI that could surpass human intelligence."

Common markers used in forecasting:
- Passes most professional-level exams and benchmarks
- Can autonomously conduct novel scientific research
- Can autonomously write and deploy software at senior-engineer level
- Self-improvement capability

## AI 2027 methodology

From [[sources/ai-2027]]:
- ~25 tabletop exercises (wargames)
- Feedback from 100+ experts across AI governance and technical fields
- Trend extrapolations from current capability curves
- Historical forecasting track record: same team predicted chain-of-thought, inference scaling, chip export controls, $100M training runs — all >1 year before ChatGPT

**Important caveat**: "Modal year" (2027) ≠ median or mean. The distribution is right-skewed, meaning long-tail scenarios push the median later.

## Scenario space

AI 2027 presents two endings:
- **Race ending** (colored red): competitive dynamics between labs lead to less controlled outcomes
- **Slowdown ending** (hopeful): more coordinated, safety-oriented path

The authors did not have a preferred ending — goal is predictive accuracy, not advocacy.

## Scale of impact claim

> "We predict that the impact of superhuman AI over the next decade will be enormous, exceeding that of the Industrial Revolution."

This is an enormity claim, not a specific economic projection. Implies: fundamental restructuring of labor, knowledge work, scientific research, geopolitics.

## Implications for current research

If timelines are even directionally correct:
- Current [[concepts/agentic-ai|agentic AI]] systems (supply chain agents, LLM wikis) are early-stage precursors.
- Knowledge management patterns (like the [[concepts/llm-wiki|LLM Wiki]]) may be substantially disrupted or enhanced within 2–3 years.
- Research on AI capabilities, safety, and governance is extremely time-sensitive.
