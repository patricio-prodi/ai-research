# Founder Traction

Evidence that a market is actively pulling a product toward it — as distinct from the founder pushing the product toward a market. Traction is the primary signal that early-stage investors use to distinguish real demand from founder hypothesis.

---

## What counts as traction

Traction is not synonymous with revenue. According to [[sources/hendrick-yc-application-tips|Hendrick's YC advice]], any of the following qualifies:

- **Active free users** — people who return and use the product without being paid to
- **Growing waitlist** — organic sign-ups indicating latent demand
- **Letters of intent (LOIs)** — formal expressions of willingness to pay
- **Early revenue** — even small amounts validate willingness to pay

The key question is: *is the market pulling this product from you, or are you pushing it at the market?*

---

## Traction vs. planning

A founding team that built an MVP in two weeks with 10 users demonstrates more about execution ability than one that spent six months writing a business plan. Traction is a proxy for velocity, judgment, and market fit — all of which compound.

---

## Relevance to YC applications

In the [[concepts/yc-application|YC application]] context, traction is one of the highest-signal elements a team can show. It doesn't need to be large — it needs to be real and growing.
