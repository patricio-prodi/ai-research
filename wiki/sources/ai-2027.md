# AI 2027

> Source: [ai-2027.com](https://ai-2027.com/) | Published 2025 (updated Nov 22 2025)

## Overview

A research-backed scenario forecast predicting that superhuman AI will arrive by approximately 2027 and that its impact will exceed that of the Industrial Revolution. Written by researchers with experience at [[entities/openai|OpenAI]], informed by ~25 tabletop exercises and feedback from 100+ experts. The document presents one of many possible futures — not a recommendation — and invites public disagreement and alternative scenarios.

## Key claims

1. **Modal year for AGI: 2027** (at time of publication; medians somewhat longer due to right-skewed distribution). Authors now maintain updated forecasts at aifuturesmodel.com.

2. **Industry consensus aligns**: CEOs of [[entities/openai|OpenAI]] (Sam Altman), Google DeepMind (Demis Hassabis), and [[entities/anthropic|Anthropic]] (Dario Amodei) have all predicted AGI within 5 years as of early 2025.

3. **Two endings written**: a "slowdown" ending and a "race" ending — both starting from the same premises. Neither is advocated; the goal is predictive accuracy.

4. **Methodology**: trend extrapolations, wargames, expert feedback, prior forecasting track record. One author successfully predicted chain-of-thought, inference scaling, AI chip export controls, and $100M training runs — all more than a year before ChatGPT (August 2021 blog post).

5. **Scale of impact claim**: "exceeding that of the Industrial Revolution" — framed as an enormity claim, not a specific economic projection.

6. **Yoshua Bengio endorsement**: "I highly recommend reading this scenario-type prediction... this type of content can help notice important questions and illustrate the potential impact of emerging risks."

## Scenario structure

- Written iteratively: period by period, then scrapped and rewritten.
- First ending (red/race) written first; alternative (hopeful/slowdown) branch added later.
- Concrete and quantitative where possible — deliberately avoids vague claims.

## Implications

- If even directionally correct, AGI timelines are short (1–3 years from the time of this article's creation).
- The scenario legitimizes treating AI capability forecasting as serious empirical work, not speculation.
- The "race" ending reflects concerns about competitive dynamics between labs — relevant to understanding [[entities/anthropic|Anthropic]]'s safety positioning.

## Caveats

- Prediction is inherently uncertain; authors acknowledge this explicitly.
- Single scenario presented as "best guess" — the actual future space is much wider.
- Institutional bias possible: ex-OpenAI authors may systematically overestimate OpenAI's trajectory.
- "Modal year" ≠ median or mean — interpreting the 2027 claim requires understanding the full distribution.
